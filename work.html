01Art Café · Belgrade

Pena Art Cafe

A room where strangers become regulars.

The problem

Belgrade has no shortage of cafés — but few that feel like a home for the people just arriving in it. Expats, travelers and newcomers rarely find a warm “third place” where they instantly belong.

What we did

We built Pena Art Cafe from the ground up — concept, brand and experience — blending specialty coffee, cocktails, comedy nights and international meetups into one welcoming space in central Belgrade, designed to turn first-time visitors into regulars.

The result

A loyal community and a perfect 5.0★ rating from 65 Google reviews — proof that people don’t just visit once, they come back.

02Space Booking · Web

Kreela

Empty hours, put to work.

The problem

Belgrade's cafés sit half-empty for hours every day, while people running workshops, clubs and language exchanges struggle to find an affordable room for a weekly slot. There was no simple way for the two to find each other.

What we did

We designed and built Kreela, a booking platform where anyone can reserve a café or small space by the hour, every week or for a full day. The venue confirms within 24 hours, the price is clear up front, and venues earn from hours that used to sit empty.

The result

Live in beta in Belgrade. Pena Art Cafe is the first venue joining, and more spaces are being added now.

  • Product design
  • Brand identity
  • Web platform
  • Booking flow

03Comedy Community · Web / PWA

ForaComedy.Club

Belgrade stand-up, all in one place.

The problem

Belgrade’s stand-up scene was scattered across social media — audiences missed shows and comedians struggled to reach them. There was no single place to see what’s on.

What we did

We designed and built ForaComedy.Club, a free online platform that brings the city’s whole stand-up scene into one hub — where audiences discover shows and the community grows.

The result

Live and already growing — onboarding Belgrade’s comedians and venues week by week.
