Kontakt forme

Kada pošaljete formu, šaljete nam podatke koje ste upisali: ime, imejl ili telefon, vrstu biznisa i opis onoga što vam treba.

  • Zašto: da vam odgovorimo i pripremimo ponudu.
  • Pravni osnov: preduzimanje radnji na vaš zahtev pre zaključenja ugovora (član 12. stav 1. tačka 2) Zakona o zaštiti podataka o ličnosti).
  • Kako stiže do nas: forma se šalje preko servisa Web3Forms, koji je prosleđuje na naš imejl kod Proton Mail-a. Ako slanje ne uspe, ili ako izaberete WhatsApp, poruku sami šaljete iz svoje WhatsApp aplikacije (Meta).
  • Koliko čuvamo: do 12 meseci od poslednjeg kontakta ako ne sarađujemo. Ako postanete klijent, onoliko koliko nalažu propisi o računovodstvu i porezima.

Šta se čuva u vašem pregledaču

Sajt pamti izabrani jezik i svetlu ili tamnu temu u lokalnoj memoriji vašeg pregledača (pena_lang, pena_theme). To ostaje na vašem uređaju, ne šalje se nama i nije kolačić. Možete ga obrisati brisanjem podataka sajta u pregledaču.

Demo aplikacije

Sve što upišete u demo salona ili teretane ostaje u vašem pregledaču i nestaje kada zatvorite ili osvežite stranicu. Ništa se ne šalje nama. Ako ste demo otvorili preko linka sa nazivom vašeg biznisa, taj naziv je deo samog linka; mi ga ne čuvamo.

Tehničke usluge koje vide vaš IP

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., SAD) hostuje sajt i, kao svaki server, beleži IP adrese radi bezbednosti.
  • Google Fonts (Google, SAD): pregledač preuzima fontove sa Google servera, koji pri tome vide vašu IP adresu.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, SAD) broji posete bez kolačića i bez praćenja pojedinaca. Vidimo samo zbirne brojeve, na primer koliko je ljudi otvorilo stranicu.

Ovi servisi, kao i Web3Forms, Proton Mail i WhatsApp, mogu da obrađuju podatke van Srbije, uključujući SAD. Ne koristimo automatsko odlučivanje ni profilisanje i ne prodajemo podatke.

Vaša prava

Imate pravo da tražite uvid u svoje podatke, njihovu ispravku ili brisanje, ograničenje obrade i prenosivost, kao i da uložite prigovor. Pišite nam na penastudio@proton.me; odgovaramo u roku od 30 dana.

Ako mislite da obrađujemo vaše podatke nezakonito, možete podneti pritužbu Povereniku za informacije od javnog značaja i zaštitu podataka o ličnosti (poverenik.rs).

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

In short: we only collect what you send us through a contact form or message, we use it only to reply to you, and we never sell it. The site sets no cookies.

Who handles your data

Ilgaz & Smirnova DOO, Belgrade, Serbia
Tax ID (PIB) 114869873
Email: penastudio@proton.me

This policy covers all of penastudio.cc, including Pena Tools at penastudio.cc/tools.

Contact forms

When you send a form, you send us what you typed: your name, email or phone, type of business and a description of what you need.

  • Why: to reply to you and prepare an offer.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 12(1)(2) of the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection).
  • How it reaches us: the form is sent through Web3Forms, which forwards it to our Proton Mail inbox. If sending fails, or if you choose WhatsApp, you send the message yourself from your WhatsApp app (Meta).
  • How long we keep it: up to 12 months after our last contact if we don’t work together. If you become a client, as long as accounting and tax rules require.

What your browser stores

The site remembers your language and light or dark theme in your browser’s local storage (pena_lang, pena_theme). This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is not a cookie. Clearing the site’s data in your browser removes it.

Demo apps

Anything you type into the salon or gym demo stays in your browser and is gone when you close or reload the page. Nothing is sent to us. If you opened a demo through a link with your business name in it, that name is part of the link itself; we don’t store it.

Technical services that see your IP address

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts the site and, like any web server, logs IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts (Google, USA): your browser downloads the fonts from Google’s servers, which see your IP address when it does.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, USA) counts visits without cookies and without tracking individuals. We only see totals, such as how many people opened a page.

These services, as well as Web3Forms, Proton Mail and WhatsApp, may process data outside Serbia, including in the USA. We don’t use automated decision-making or profiling, and we don’t sell data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, restrict its processing, receive a copy, or object to its use. Email penastudio@proton.me; we reply within 30 days.

If you believe we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to Serbia’s Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (poverenik.rs).

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will be on this page with a new date at the top.
